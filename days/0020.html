Day 20: The Number Still Saved as Tamsin

Wednesday 18 July 2046, morning

Year 10 science lessons move to the hall from 22 July. Please send your child with a coat.Linden Rise School parents' newsletter

Two days on in the north lanes, a letter from Ines Marchetti, the caseworker, told Elspeth Hale her witness interview at the Continuity office was due. Constance Webb wanted to sit in. Constance had once asked her to tell a girl she was dead; she would be in the room, and the file still wanted Natalie's name. Elspeth did not cry off, so the file would have a witness who came. She took the register in at the arm where the blight held the ground.

Up the lanes Isabel sat coatless on a step the thaw missed, locked out, no key, no phone. The stone drew the cold into her hands and chest.

On the steps at Isabel's flat, Natalie kept Iz's phone pocketed and the door shut, so Iz would have to come to her.

Outside the old woman's house in the eastern wards, Hyejin Park's phone lit up: Tamsin, a number she had never deleted. The caller was Tamsin's sister, training to teach at Linden Rise. A Year 10 girl hid her forearm the way Tamsin had; the crawler went in under her classroom on the 22nd.

Says it like saying it stops it, Hyejin thought. Mention an arm kindly and it's sorted.

She hung up so there would be no next question, and powered the phone down.
