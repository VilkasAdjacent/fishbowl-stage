Day 4: The Girl Who Held the Door Shut

Thursday 5 July 2046, night

Plant into the burn.a Second Growth sticker on a bus shelter

At the desk Isabel asked again about the kiss.

Natalie did not answer. She took Isabel's coat from the chair and the phone from the desk and carried them into the bathroom. The door stuck on its swollen frame. She set both palms flat against it and pushed until the lock caught. The burn opened under the wrap and wept through. Afterward she could not close her fingers.

Before morning the ash under the fire stair, left from Isabel's blaze-out, sent green into every dead planter box and cracked grate in the lane. Stems came up through the drains. Buds opened in midwinter. Neighbours came down in raincoats and slippers and held their phones over it. The photos went into Isabel's chat one after another. Behind the bathroom door the phone lit blue and buzzed against the tiles.

In the north lanes the blight in the brick grew nerves again.

Isabel went down with no coat. The lane was full of people and leaves. She crouched at the first planter and did not get up for a long time. The drizzle had turned to rain. It soaked her to the skin. When she climbed back up she was shaking harder than when she had gone down. Her hands had stiffened in the cold and would not hold the rail.

Off Linden Rise, Amara Nwosu followed a buzz down into the wet dark. She told no one. She came out in a lane she did not know, shoes full of water. Nothing had been cut.

Hyejin Park wrote to a novice girl. Three messages: what it costs, how girls die, that nobody is coming. The girl started a reply and stopped partway through a sentence. Nothing else came from that handle. Hyejin had her own three messages on the screen. The girl stayed home.

    • Isabel now believes: Natalie locked her coat and her phone in the bathroom rather than answer, so the kiss is the answer — Natalie has decided it was a mistake, and so is she.
    • Natalie now believes: Locking the phone in the bathroom bought a night, not an answer; Iz will read the door as the answer and act on it.
