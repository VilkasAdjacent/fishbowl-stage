Day 12: The Girl Who Stood Behind the Bin Store Until Four

Monday 9 July 2046, morning

Linden Rise: all year groups dismissed 10:00 due to flooding. Please confirm your child has arrived home safely.a text from the school office to parents

On the ninth, meltwater backed up through the estate drains at Linden Rise and flooded the quad and lower corridors. By ten every year group had been sent home.

Amara Nwosu took the free day down the lake path, the moth mascot riding her hood. At the standard she made the cut alone. It was more than one pair of hands could hold, and it got away from her. She came up the bank with her sleeve torn open and copper drying in the cuts.

Her mother's messages about the flood kept coming. She left them unanswered and went the back way toward her room. The drains had taken the footpath. She waded in to the waist, felt it still rising, and turned back.

Every message had a right answer, and every answer was a door. Coming in wet at four could be asked about. Standing still could not.

She stood out of the wind behind the bin store. Her hands went numb, then shook, and the shivering took the rest of her and did not stop. She never got in.

At the eastern wards Elspeth Hale told Hyejin Park the north lanes were clear. She said it level. Hyejin let out a breath and took it, and the line in the register stayed blank.

"Hyejin Park, if I might—"

"No. Fuck off. Leave me alone."

Hyejin shut the door. Elspeth Hale stood on the step a moment, then left.
