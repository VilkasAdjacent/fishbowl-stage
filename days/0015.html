Day 15: The Girl Who Was Asked Kindly on the Back Step

Thursday 12 July 2046, dawn

Seen something? Say something. Somebody else saw it too.the banner on the Bay Sightings app

Next dawn in the eastern wards, Hyejin Park, forty-one, stopped at nineteen for eleven years and running again since, was at the beds when the old woman came out onto the back step in her dressing gown. She had the tablet, Dev Okafor's video paused on Hyejin's turned face.

"Is that you, love? Were you at the block, the night it went green?"

Nobody filming, nothing unkind to refuse. Eleven years spent so this was never asked.

Hyejin stood in the mulch with the spade. Then she drove it into the frozen crust and worked on as if she had not heard. If she never looked up, the question stayed on the step, asked once.

At the Linden Rise drain lake, Amara Nwosu wrote privately to the unknown handle from the Bay Sightings thread: what did you see at the lake? It was the first thing she had asked anyone since this began. The culvert held more than she could cut alone, and this stranger had seen it.

Elspeth Hale, a hundred and twenty, stopped at seventeen, stood by with the register, where each blight was entered against the line that answered it. She looked at the lake, then at Amara, and wrote. The drain lake's line ran to this girl, three weeks old at most, and the thing under the culvert was larger than so young a line should carry alone. Telling her would not change the entry, so Elspeth did not.

Amara watched the pen move.
