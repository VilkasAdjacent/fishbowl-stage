Day 1: The Girl Who Walked a Half Step Ahead

Tuesday 3 July 2046, night

Lower Bridge shelter closed for repairs until further notice. Please allow extra time.a River Tram service notice

At four in the morning the downstairs neighbour sent a message. Hoshi had been yowling at the same corner of the flat for an hour. Come and deal with your cat.

Isabel was dressed before she woke Natalie. She said the cat needed her. She named the bridge she would take and how long it would be. She said nothing else. Her keys were already in her hand.

Natalie got up and put on her coat. No one had asked her to. She said she would walk with her, and held the door.

The rain came off the river sideways. The south ridge towers blinked red through it. They crossed at the lower bridge, where there was no shelter. Natalie's coat was not made for weather. By the second street it was black with water at the shoulders and cuffs, and her shoes had filled. She put her hands in her pockets. They did not stop shaking.

Isabel walked a half step ahead the whole way.
